ℹ️ About HarvestAlert

Our purpose

HarvestAlert is an educational smart-farming platform designed to help farmers understand weather conditions, water availability, crop planning, harvest estimates and agricultural resources.

It combines actual weather forecasts with farmer-provided information and clearly labelled planning estimates.
