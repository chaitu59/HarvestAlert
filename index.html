ℹ️ About this prototype

Open-Meteo supplies the weather forecast. Risk scores are simplified screening indicators, not official warnings. The crop-health checker does not confirm a disease. Harvest ranges are illustrative, and profit depends on the values you enter. Verify prices, crop treatment and scheme eligibility with official sources or your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK). Do not apply pesticides based only on this tool.
